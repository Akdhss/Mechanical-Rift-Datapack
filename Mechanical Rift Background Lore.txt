
[ Background ]
The Mechanical Rift is formed from the ruin of a kingdom long ago that had a device that was capable of recreating any entity and terrain that it had the blueprint to. While this device was used to the betterment of their own people, the lingering eyes of other parties seeked the device for their own gain. 

And so, the kingdom was forced under the banner of war. Their attacks were relentless but the kingdom stood tall and kept on surviving under the leadership of the Three Founders, but not even they could keep on fighting forever. To keep the device safe from the people who want to keep it for themselves, the last remaining member of the Three Founders reprogrammed the machine to protect itself to preserve the kingdom's last legacy.

After the onslaught of time and millenia, rot took hold of the device's mechanisms and forced it to stray from its path and kept endlessly summoning the souls of the dead to rise again to defend itself from a threat non-existent.

This caused the Adventurers Guild's eyes to turn to the place where they have not placed before and placed a commission to any party willing to explore and put the damned souls back to their peaceful rest.

However the Mechanical Rift is no peaceful vacation. Upon entering its domain, all of the user's code and physical prowess gets stripped away from them for the entire time they stay within the Rift's domain. Not all hope is lost however as its main function had been kept alive even through the years. As the user keeps sending the souls to rest, the now freed souls grant a little bit of their power to the one who freed them. Using the power they have gotten, the user can obtain the power of fallen warriors who had died to protect the kingdom.

Go forth Adventurers.



[ Code ]


[ Aragnos Kingdom ]
This was a powerful kingdom long ago born from the coalescence of multiple refugee groups that seeked shelter from the invasion long before the nation's inception.  Notably, there are three major groups that primarly make up the original populace of the Aragorn Kingdom. Each group was led by a primary figure that ended up being known instead as the Three Founders of the Aragorn Kingdom. To accomodate the large groups of refugees in the land they had settled in, certain areas were designated and given to each group to live in. Local matters where dealt within they're own circles however any matters that involve more than 1 refugee group will be taken notice and supervised by any one of the Three Founders. Overtime, their stake on the land had given them a new place to call home and had begun formalizing their claim of the land.

The Aragnos Kingdom was prosperous beyond measure. Technology so advanced that it was indistinguishable from magic and coffers so filled that gold was as abundant as wood. Even if the kingdom themselves were prosperous, they did not seek to wage war as they remember where this nation's roots come from.. But that was not the same manifesto of those who look upon this golden kingdom with envious eyes.


[ Three Founders ]
The Three Founders are the reason why the kingdom exists in the first place. Not much is known from them but it is known that they are worshipped as the protective deities of their kingdom. There are also records that show they also existed alongside their people and guided rulers by their side. From what we know, each Founder had their own role to play.

These are the names of the Three Founders from what the researchers could decipher.

-> Matt [ The God Slayer ] 
-> Vita [ The Hearth ]
-> Karel [ The Architect ]


[ Matt ]
They are suspected to be one of the Three Founders of the Aragnos Kingdom and are believed to be the being that symbolizes combat and strength. Records exist of "Matt" that show he taught the populace the way of combat and had given birth to an unique fighting style that the army of the kingdom used prevalently until its fall. There is speculation that "Matt" had the least talent on code manipulation between the Three Founders but is on equal if not higher footing in terms of combat due to his skill alone.


[ Vita ]
They are suspected to be one of the Three Founders of the Aragnos Kingdom and are believed to be the being that symbolizes life and wisdom. Records exist of "Vita" that show that she had possessed powerful code that allowed mechanical beings to spring to life without making them sentient. Several other advanced machines have been noted to be made by her that shows her prowess of her control over her powers.


[ Karel ]  
They are suspected to be one of the Three Founders of the Aragnos Kingsom and are believed to be the being that symbolizes home and order. Records exist of "Karel" that show that she was not a biological being like "Vita" and "Matt" are but possess a will and a sentient mind unlike the creations of "Vita". Not much is known about her aside from all of the infrastructure and buildings in the Aragnos Kingdom was built by her hand. It is unknown whether she possessed a code that helped with the construction of the kingdom or if this was all just built using skill alone.
